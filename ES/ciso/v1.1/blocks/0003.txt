# CLAUDE.md — educational GRC context, not proof of implementation

## Regulatory input
- GDPR (Regulation 2016/679), LOPDGDD, ENS (RD 311/2022)
- NIS2, DORA and AI Act: assess role, scope, jurisdiction and current timetable
- MAGERIT v3 is a risk methodology, not a law
- ISO text requires the appropriate edition and usage rights

## Mapping and validation
1. Read the complete, current primary provision and retain its source.
2. Map legal requirements, applicability conditions and separate operational fields.
3. Use NOT NULL or activation validators only where the assessed condition requires data.
4. Validate enumerations and cross-field rules; distinguish GDPR Articles 6 and 9.
5. Generate drafts, then review expert interpretation and run meaningful tests.

## Security contracts
- Authenticate each protected boundary with its approved JWT, SAML, LDAP or PKI path.
- Authorize action and object; scope every read, write, join, cache and job to trusted context.
- Test tenant separation and atomic row-version conflicts.
- Apply lawful retention, access restriction and actual erasure; soft delete is not erasure.
- Record approved minimal audit events and validate coverage; do not log secrets or raw PII.
- UUIDs reduce enumeration but never replace authorization.
- Verify encryption, key handling and TLS per component.
- Proprietary core and Desktop remain private permanently. Public examples are educational.
