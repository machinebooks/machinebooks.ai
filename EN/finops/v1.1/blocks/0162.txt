Reference process; needs actual configuration and evidence:
1. Propose the change on a branch with reasons and scope.
2. Validate schema, ranges, sovereignty, eligible models and impact in comparable windows.
3. Obtain approval bound to the exact diff and scope; invalidate it when the diff changes.
4. Protect the branch and deploy a digest-identified immutable artifact to staging.
5. Evaluate staging against coverage criteria; fifteen minutes does not prove production.
6. Promote the same artifact with authorization and retain receipt, version and rollback.
A merge or tag proves neither approval, application nor control effectiveness.
