Reference rollback process:
1. Identify the applied artifact and previous approved version.
2. Prepare the revert on a branch; review conflicts and the complete diff.
3. Validate policy, expiry, sovereignty and data/schema compatibility.
4. Approve and deploy the previous artifact through the protected process.
5. Verify application and reconcile decisions/costs during transition.
A git revert or push does not prove recovery or bypass approval.
