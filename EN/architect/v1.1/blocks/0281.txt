# Generic prompt (insufficient)
"Write a user manual for the document analysis module."

# Context-engineered prompt (effective)
"Generate the 'Document Analysis' section of the Platform user manual,
aimed at a business analyst with average experience in digital tools.

Reader profile: non-technical, works with client requirements documents,
needs to extract key information from lengthy documents (50-200 pages) to
prepare commercial proposals. Their goal is not to understand how the AI works but
to complete their task faster.

Main user flow:
1. Uploads a PDF or Word document from the 'Analysis' tab
2. The system automatically extracts requirements, deadlines, and evaluation criteria
3. The user reviews the results, corrects if necessary, and exports

Include: step-by-step guide with textually described screenshots, common errors
and how to resolve them, frequently asked questions based on real support tickets.
DO NOT use technical jargon. Refer to the system as 'the Platform'.
Tone: professional and accessible, without condescension."
