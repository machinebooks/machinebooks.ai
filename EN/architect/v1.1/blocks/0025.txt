Prompt for detecting inconsistencies in requirements:

"These are the steps of the business process I want to automate:
[list of steps]

Act as a systems architect. Identify:
1. Dependencies between steps that could create bottlenecks
2. Steps that can fail and block the entire flow
3. Data needed in multiple steps — can it be shared?
4. Steps with very different technical characteristics (latency, concurrency, security)
5. Implicit assumptions that may be false in production

Don't give me solutions yet. Only identify the problems so I
can decide how to address them."
