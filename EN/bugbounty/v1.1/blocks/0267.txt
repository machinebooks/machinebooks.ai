1. Client process opens \\.\pipe\asuscert
2. Client sends 4 bytes: DWORD with its own PID
3. AsusCertService receives the PID
4. AsusCertService gets executable path from PID
   (via NtQueryInformationProcess or GetModuleFileNameEx)
5. Runs WinVerifyTrust on the executable path
6. Verifies signing certificate belongs to ASUS
7. If valid: responds "OK!" (3 bytes) + adds PID to list
8. If fails: closes connection without response
9. AsIO3.sys driver queries the authorized PID list
   before processing each IOCTL
