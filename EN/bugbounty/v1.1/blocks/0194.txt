LPE HYPOTHESIS — requires installation-specific validation:
1. Determine effective token writing to the relevant code path.
2. Identify the service account and the path it actually consumes.
3. Verify effective startup, stop or configuration rights.
4. Check integrity, signatures, repair and other loading restrictions.
5. In an authorized fixture, observe a benign marker and the context producing it.
6. Record controls and conditions breaking the chain.
Do not replace binaries or restart real services as part of this example.
Severity and the CVSS vector depend on demonstrated impact.
