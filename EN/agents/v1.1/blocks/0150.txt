sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    participant L as Parent AgentLoop
    participant M as SubAgentManager
    participant G as A2AGovernance
    participant C as Child (child AgentLoop)
    participant N as TaskNotificationManager

    L->>M: spawnAgent(type, prompt)
    M->>M: register in sessions / pool
    M->>C: runAgent() (async)
    M-->>L: agentId (returns immediately)
    Note over L: the parent continues its turn
    C->>C: internal ReAct loop
    C-->>M: final result
    M->>G: record(A2AEvent {tokens, latency, status})
    M->>N: enqueue(taskNotification)
    L->>L: next turn
    L->>N: flushPending(messages)
    N-->>L: injects <task-notification> as user msg
    L->>L: the LLM sees the child's result
