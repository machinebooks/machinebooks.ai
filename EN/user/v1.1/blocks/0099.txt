Generate a 10-slide presentation to convince the steering committee
to approve this project.

Structure of a persuasive presentation:

SLIDE 1: Cover with project title

SLIDE 2: The problem
- What problem does this project solve?
- Who does it affect?
- How much does doing nothing cost us? (concrete figure)

SLIDE 3: The proposed solution
- Description in 3-4 points
- What will change when it is implemented

SLIDE 4: Expected benefits
- Estimated ROI
- Time/cost savings
- Qualitative benefits

SLIDE 5: Scope and phases
- What it includes and what it does NOT include
- Project phases with duration

SLIDE 6: Timeline
- Visual timeline (table with months and phases)

SLIDE 7: Budget
- Breakdown by line item
- Comparison with the cost of doing nothing

SLIDE 8: Risks and mitigation
- 3 main risks
- Mitigation plan for each

SLIDE 9: Team and governance
- Who leads, who participates
- How decisions are made

SLIDE 10: Approval request
- What exactly is being requested (budget, resources, timeline)
- Next steps if approved
- Deadline for the decision

Tone: professional, action-oriented, with concrete data.
Do not sell — convince with facts.
Each slide with 2-3 sentence presenter notes.
