When I ask you to generate weekly team meeting minutes,
always use this format:

1. Header: date, attendees, duration
2. Previous week's commitment review (fulfilled / pending)
3. Topics discussed with highlighted decisions
4. New actions: table with responsible person, description, date
5. 3-line executive summary
6. Next meeting

For client meetings, add a "detected risks" section and always
generate an external version (for the client) and an internal
one (for the team).
