3. Classify all expenses according to the mapping in mapeo-categorias-2024.json

4. Compare with the monthly budget (presupuesto-2024.xlsx: identify and confirm the March sheet/column)

5. Generate:
   - Reconciliation report with pending transactions
   - Monthly variance report
   - Comparison with previous months (January and February)
   - List of pending actions (unreconciled transactions, unclassified expenses)

6. Save copies in a new folder under cierres/2024-03/; do not overwrite
   earlier reports. Do not post adjustments or approve the close.
