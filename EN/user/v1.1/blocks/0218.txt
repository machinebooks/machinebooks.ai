When prioritizing my tasks, use these levels:
- LEVEL 1 (immovable): client commitments, management deadlines,
  meetings with external parties
- LEVEL 2 (unblocks others): tasks that another team member
  depends on
- LEVEL 3 (important without urgency): individual work,
  training, process improvement

Always distribute levels 1 and 2 first. Level 3 fills the
remaining gaps.
