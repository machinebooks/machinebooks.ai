# Monthly financial reporting pipeline

## Example context
Technology services company: eight employees and approximately 1.2 million EUR annual invoicing.
The CSVs in datos/ are example sources; preserve their provenance and period.

## Calculation rules
- EUR with Decimal and two decimal places; no currency conversion without a source and date
- Issued counts toward issued invoicing; report Draft separately
- Paid identifies a cash outflow; Pending is an outstanding obligation
- Do not confuse those states with accounting recognition of income or expense
- Reconcile ERP, bank, cards, credits, and partial payments before aggregation
- Compare budget and actual on the same basis and category
- Deviation: (actual - budget) / budget * 100, when budget is nonzero
- Do not call invoiced amounts minus paid expenses operating margin

## Format and acceptance
Markdown with sources and tables; European format 12.500,00 EUR and 85,3%.
Executive summary, invoices, collections, expenses by state, budget, and recommendations.
Record missing data and errors; stop final delivery on a missing critical source.
Open and check the complete output before human review; do not send it automatically.
- Q1 budget and CSVs are 2025 fixtures; they do not establish current data.
- No due date or unique invoice ID is supplied: do not classify overdue invoices.
- For this fixture, reconcile only (client, concept) with a unique match.
- Collections do not prove accrued income, nor does issued invoicing prove accrual.
