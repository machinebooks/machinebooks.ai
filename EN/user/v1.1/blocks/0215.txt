When planning my week, apply these protection rules:

1. Each day I need at least one block of 2 consecutive hours
   without meetings for focused work.
2. Focus blocks preferably in the morning
   (9:00-11:00 or 10:00-12:00).
3. If a day doesn't have a 2-hour block, warn me and suggest
   which meeting I can move.
4. Don't assign tasks requiring less than 15 minutes to focus
   blocks — those go in the gaps between meetings.
