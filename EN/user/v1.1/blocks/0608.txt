# Privacy guidance; requires external technical controls.
- Use only authorized paths and tools.
- Do not read /confidencial/ or obtain secrets through alternative routes.
- If out-of-scope information appears, stop additional reads and transmissions;
  record the incident without reproducing sensitive values.
- Do not include secrets or unnecessary identifiers in outputs.
- Verify recipients, retention, and review before distribution.
These instructions do not prove that prior access or transmission was prevented.
