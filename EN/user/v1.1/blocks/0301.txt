| Commercial zone | March 2023 | March 2024 | Variance | % |
|-----------------|-----------|-----------|----------|---|
| Centro | 45 | 52 | +7 | +15.6% |
| Norte | 23 | 31 | +8 | +34.8% |
| Este | 31 | 28 | -3 | -9.7% |

| Internacional | 14 | 19 | +5 | +35.7% |
| Oeste | 12 | 15 | +3 | +25.0% |
| Canarias | 8 | 11 | +3 | +37.5% |
| Baleares | 5 | 7 | +2 | +40.0% |
| **Total** | **156** | **185** | **+29** | **+18.6%** |

### SQL query used:
SELECT z.nombre as zona_comercial,
  SUM(CASE WHEN c.fecha_alta >= '2023-03-01'
    AND c.fecha_alta < '2023-04-01' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as marzo_2023,
  SUM(CASE WHEN c.fecha_alta >= '2024-03-01'
    AND c.fecha_alta < '2024-04-01' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as marzo_2024
FROM clientes c
JOIN zonas z ON c.zona_comercial = z.id_zona
WHERE (c.fecha_alta >= '2023-03-01' AND c.fecha_alta < '2023-04-01')
   OR (c.fecha_alta >= '2024-03-01' AND c.fecha_alta < '2024-04-01')
GROUP BY z.nombre
ORDER BY marzo_2024 DESC;

### Observations:
- Overall year-over-year growth of 18.6%
- All zones are growing except Este (-9.7%)
- Highest relative growth in Baleares (+40%) and Canarias (+37.5%)
- Highest absolute growth in Norte (+8) and Centro (+7)
