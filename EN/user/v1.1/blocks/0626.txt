# CLAUDE.md — Monthly close

## My role
Financial controller. Responsible for the monthly close and reporting to management.

## Data sources
- ERP: CSV export of accounting entries (datos/erp/)
- Bank: statements in OFX or CSV format (datos/banco/)
- Expenses: employee expense sheets in Excel (datos/gastos/)
- Invoices: vendor invoices in PDF (datos/facturas/)

## Report format
- Always in euros, two decimals, European format
- Report structure: see plantillas/informe-cierre.md
- Charts with corporate colors: blue (#003366), gray (#666666), green (#339966)

## Security rules
- Data contains confidential financial information
- Verify tools, inference, destinations, and contracts before processing; a prompt does not prevent transmission
- Process locally only with a client, model, and routes verified for that scope
- Pseudonymize employee names in intermediate reports and protect the mapping; do not claim anonymization where reidentification remains possible
