ILLUSTRATIVE NETWORK DIAGNOSIS:

Both containers are assumed on staging_default and DNS resolves
redis to 172.18.0.4; the connection to redis:6379 is refused.
That alone does not establish bind 127.0.0.1 as the cause.
Check the process, port, effective configuration, network, and time
with authorized permissions. A local PING does not prove api-backend access.

If loopback-only listening is confirmed to block a legitimate client,
review an authorized private interface or network together with ACLs,
authentication, protected mode, and TLS where applicable.
Do not switch to bind 0.0.0.0 as an automatic fix or publish 6379
without controlling exposure. Repeat tests from the authorized client
before treating connectivity as resolved.
