# Template: Quarterly invoice extraction
# Save as: D:\Templates\invoice_instructions.md

Process all PDF invoices in the folder [PATH].
Extract: vendor, tax ID, invoice number, date, description, base, VAT rate,
VAT amount, total, payment method.
CSV format with semicolon separator.
Dates in DD/MM/YYYY. Amounts with 2 decimals, period as decimal separator.
If a field is not readable: "NOT READABLE".
Generate summary at the end with totals and list of invoices to review.
Before execution, fill in [PATH], period, currency, rules and a new destination.
Do not use filename or file date as the sole accounting criterion.
Preserve originals and page references; inventory every input.
Document multiple VAT rates and total components without losing their links.
Separate missing, unreadable and ambiguous values; do not replace them with zero.
Check critical fields, coverage, calculations and exceptions.
Do not distribute or submit tax returns from the draft.
