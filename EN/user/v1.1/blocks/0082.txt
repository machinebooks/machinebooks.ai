March 2024 sales amounted to 487,320 €, a 12.3% increase over
February (433,940 €). This growth is concentrated mainly in the
Southern region (+23.1%), according to the March-versus-February comparison. The product with the highest growth was the Premium line
(+18.7%), while the Basic line fell by 4.2%. 14 customers without recorded February sales were identified, representing 8% of the month's revenue; that absence does not establish they are new customers.
