I analyzed gastos-departamentos-2024.xlsx and generated 4 visualizations:

1. **Total monthly trend** (line chart)
   Chosen because the data is a time series with 12 points.
   Shows the overall spending trend.

2. **Composition by department** (stacked bars, month by month)
   Chosen because there are multiple categories that sum to a total.
   Allows seeing how each department's proportion changes.

3. **Department ranking** (horizontal bars with total amount)
   Chosen to facilitate direct comparison between departments.
   Sorted from highest to lowest spending.

4. **Spending heatmap** (heat map: departments × months)
   Chosen because with 7 departments and 12 months, a heatmap allows
   identifying at a glance where spending is concentrated and whether
   there are seasonal patterns by department.

All 4 charts are saved as individual PNGs and also
combined in an interactive HTML (dashboard-gastos-2024.html).
